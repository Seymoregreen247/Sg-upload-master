SG Mockup Maker V5 Touch

Upload → Position → Download
Default: background stays exactly as uploaded. Turn on Remove background only when you want it. Choose Auto edge color, White, or Black and adjust tolerance if a border remains. On each garment, tap the artwork to edit it, drag it into position, and use the green corner handle to resize it. Then tap Lock. Locked artwork cannot move and the green outline disappears. Swipe outside unlocked artwork—or anywhere on a locked garment—to scroll. Each garment is independent. Downloads are automatically cropped and re-centered around the garment so there is no loose canvas space for the mockup to appear to slide.
