SG MOCKUP MAKER — NETLIFY PWA

1. Unzip this package.
2. In Netlify, use Deploy manually / drag-and-drop.
3. Drag the SG_Mockup_Maker_Netlify_PWA folder (or all of its contents) into Netlify.
4. index.html must be at the published site root.
5. After deploy, open the HTTPS Netlify URL once while online.
6. On Android/Chrome use Add to Home screen / Install app.
7. The Kung Fu man holding the shirt is the favicon/PWA icon.

Do not upload index.html alone. Keep the assets folder, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, _redirects and netlify.toml with it.
